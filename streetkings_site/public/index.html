About

A city that runs on gasoline

StreetKings is a car community first. Every system in the city is built around the cars: where you find them, how you fix them, how you tune them and where you show them off.

Pull a wreck off the scrapyard lot, strip it down bolt by bolt, put it back together and register it. Then take it to the strip, a meet or the mountains. Your car, your build, your story.

  • Real wearParts, tyres and fluids wear out and need a mechanic
  • Player runShops, garages and businesses owned by players
  • Serious RPPolice, EMS and mechanics played by real people
  • Fair economyEarned money, server checked, no shortcuts

The city

What you can do

01

Project cars

Buy a wreck from the scrapyard, find its hidden faults, strip it and rebuild it into a street car.

02

Tuning and stance

Engine, gearbox and suspension upgrades, plus stance and fitment to get the look just right.

03

Drag strip

Line up, launch and run the timer. Settle it at the strip instead of on the highway.

04

Real mechanics

Mechanic shops repair, tune and bill for their work. Neglect your car and it will let you know.

05

Run a business

Open a shop, hire your crew, set your prices and keep the books. The city runs on players.

06

Meets and crews

Car meets, cruises and crews. Bring the build out and let the city see it.
