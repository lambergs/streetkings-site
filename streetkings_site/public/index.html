About StreetKings

A city built around the car.

StreetKings is a car community first. Where you find a car, how you fix it, how you tune it and where you show it off are all part of the roleplay.

Around the cars is a full city: shops run by players, police and medics on duty, jobs to grind and places to hang out. Come for the builds, stay for the people.

  • Real wearParts, tyres and fluids wear out and need a mechanic.
  • Player runShops, garages and businesses are owned and staffed by players.
  • Earned, not givenMoney and progress are checked by the server. No shortcuts.

Features

Everything the city runs on.

Every item below is a system running on the server today.

Project cars

Buy a wreck from the scrapyard, find its hidden faults, strip it bolt by bolt and rebuild it into a registered street car.

Tuning and stance

Engine, gearbox and suspension upgrades, plus stance and fitment work to get the look exactly right.

Drag strips

Staging beams, a Christmas tree, timeslips and ranked boards. Bracket racing, buy-ins, bets and tournaments.

Mechanic shops

Parts wear out. Mechanics diagnose, repair, tune and bill for the work, and a neglected car lets you know.

Manual gearbox

Row your own gears with a shift light on the dash. Your launches and shifts show up on the timeslip.

Player-owned businesses

Open a shop, hire a crew, set the menu and prices, run the register and pay your staff from the books.

Police and EMS

Officers and medics are players on duty, with dispatch calls, a full station and a working hospital.

Jobs and hustles

Fishing, mining, fruit picking, recycling and towing. Honest work that pays for the next set of parts.

Life off the road

Basketball, pool, pickleball, paintball, the gym, videoke, the casino, camping and a house of your own.

    City rules

    Know them before you play.

    Everyone gets the same city. Tap a rule to read it.

    01OOC (out of character)
    • Stay in character while in the city.
    • Do not talk about real-life or OOC topics during RP.
    02Voice recognition
    • You cannot identify someone just by their voice if they are wearing a mask or helmet.
    03Combat logging
    • Do not leave, disconnect or quit during RP or combat.
    04Meta gaming
    • Do not use information from outside the game to help your character.
    • Do not use Discord, Facebook, streams or other apps for in-game information.
    • Do not use Toggle ID to see players through walls or tinted vehicle windows.
    05Power gaming
    • Always act realistically.
    • No talking while unconscious.
    • No using /me to force an outcome.
    • No ignoring danger or fear.
    • No cancelling or spamming emotes to gain an advantage.
    • No returning to a situation after being downed.
    • No carrying someone while running if it is unrealistic.
    • No claiming unrealistic abilities.
    06New Life Rule (NLR)
    • If you respawn after being downed, forget everything that caused you to be downed.
    • Do not return to the scene after respawning.
    • Do not force someone to respawn without a valid reason.
    • Do not use phrases like “Nag-see-the-light ako” to avoid NLR.
    • Do not keep dead bodies for more than 30 minutes.
    • You may remember what happened after checking into the hospital.
    07RDM
    • Do not randomly kill someone without a valid RP reason.
    08VDM
    • Do not intentionally use a vehicle to kill or injure someone without a valid RP reason.
    09Value of life / Fear RP
    • Your character must value their life.
    • If someone points a gun at you, comply unless you have a clear chance to escape or fight back.
    • Do not overkill downed or dead players.
    • Try RP before starting violence.
    • Do not randomly start shootouts at police stations or hospitals.
    • Do not provoke police, gangs or armed players without a valid RP reason.
    • Do not jump into situations that do not involve you.
    • After a violent situation, give others time to recover.
    • RP should stop after a tsunami.
    10Force RP
    • Do not control another player's character or force actions on them.
    • Do not force yourself into specialized roles such as police, doctor or mechanic.
    • Do not kill someone simply because they are vulnerable or distracted.
    11Common sense / Enough is enough
    • Do not treat RP like a competition.
    • Use common sense.
    • Just because something is possible in real life does not mean it is allowed in RP.
    • Keep RP fun for everyone.
    12Assuming / False accusations
    • Do not create drama based on assumptions.
    • Let Guardians and staff handle reports.
    • False accusations or claims without proof may result in a ban.
    13Cop baiting
    • Do not purposely attract police attention just to get chased.
    • No fake or spam 911 calls.
    • No unnecessary drifting, burnouts or revving near police.
    • Do not purposely break traffic laws around police to start a chase.
    14RMT (real money trading)
    • Buying or selling in-game items, cars, houses or services with real money is only allowed through official channels.
    • Unauthorized RMT will result in a permanent ban.
    15Vouching
    • Only vouch for people you actually know.
    • If someone you vouched for gets banned, you may also receive a sanction.
    • 1st offense: verbal warning and 100 minutes jail.
    16Club cars
    • If a member leaves a club while having a club car, the club leader must make a ticket.
    • Admins will reclaim the vehicle and return it to the club.
    17Sidejob robbery
    • Do not wait outside side jobs just to rob players.
    • Report anyone breaking this rule through a ticket.
    • Robbery is allowed during the evening.
    • Use the designated garages to store your vehicles.
    18Exclusive vehicles
    • No animated cars.
    • No glowing or unrealistic lighting effects.
    • Have custom car files checked by an Admin or Car Developer before paying.
    • Unchecked files may be rejected or cause vehicle problems.
    19Death from accident or hunger
    • If you die from an accident or hunger, use /callmedic. You must have a phone to use it.
    • Keep your hunger filled and take care of your character.
    • Do not use /report or Help Desk for deaths caused by your own accident or hunger.
    • For other deaths, you may contact staff through Help Desk or /report.
    • Always stay in character and accept the consequences of your actions.
    20Name change
    • Your requested name must match your in-game character name.
    • Name changes cost ₱1,000,000 in-game money.
    • Double-check your name before submitting a request.
    21Drug selling
    • Use a 4-door vehicle or a Car-D vehicle. 2-door vehicles and exclusive cars are prohibited.
    • The vehicle may have 1 to 4 occupants during the transaction.
    • Skateboards, bicycles and motorcycles are prohibited.
    • Failure to comply will result in sanctions.
